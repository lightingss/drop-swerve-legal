English

Data stored on your device

Drop & Swerve is a single-player game. The game stores the following locally on your device using Apple user defaults / Unity PlayerPrefs:

  • your language choice;
  • the eight-hour clock, ending and achievement state;
  • best runs and unlocked local achievements;
  • sound, haptic and tutorial settings;
  • local supporter points and an anonymous supporter ID.

This local data is not uploaded unless you use one of the optional features described below. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases and the supporter board

Optional developer tips and the Remove Ads purchase are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. We do not receive your name, email address, card details or Apple ID. The app receives StoreKit product identifiers, transaction identifiers and the app receipt so it can deliver the purchase and restore entitlements.

If you choose to make your support public, the game sends an anonymous supporter token, a server-generated display name, non-spendable rank points and a public/private flag to our supporter API at vsoho.work after Apple receipt verification. The server stores the transaction ID, product ID, points, Apple environment (Production or Sandbox), timestamps and the public display name. It does not store your Apple ID, email address, card details, device identifier or raw receipt. The public board shows only opted-in display names and points.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show ads and to collect consent where required. The ad SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad requests, impressions and clicks, related app events and diagnostics, for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and reliability. This data may be shared with Google and its measurement partners under their policies.

Ads are shown only after a run result or the completed ending, and an optional rewarded video may be offered on the failure screen; declining it does not block play. Where required, a Privacy Options entry in the pause menu lets you change your consent choices.

On iOS, after the consent form the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to deliver and measure personalized ads. If you deny or restrict it, the app does not access the IDFA and ads are limited to non-personalized or contextual advertising; the game remains fully playable. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Product analytics (Firebase)

The game includes Google Firebase Analytics. Collection starts disabled. It is enabled only when UMP reports that consent is not required, or that consent was obtained with IAB TCF Purpose 1 set; if consent is refused or unknown, analytics remains off.

Events cover game starts and runs, tutorial progress, settings changes, charge/drop/impact timing, obstacle passes and near misses, deaths, deliveries, revive offers and rewarded-ad outcomes, purchases and restores (product ID and result), and coarse ad impressions. Event parameters are limited to gameplay values such as mode, district, order number, object type, charge time, travel distance, success or failure reason, revive method and in-game voucher counts. A random per-session identifier groups events; it is not your anonymous player account, invite code, invite token, nickname or an advertising identifier. The app does not send Apple or Google account details, payment details, receipts, transaction IDs, contacts or chat content to Firebase. Firebase may also process an app-scoped installation identifier and operational diagnostics. The Privacy Options entry re-evaluates collection immediately when you change your choice.

Friends, invitations and revive tickets

The Friends & Rewards panel is optional. It is not a social login and does not ask for your phone number, email address, contact list or social-media account. On first use, the game creates one anonymous player identity on our server. The server stores an invite code, your friendships, who invited whom, whether a new player completed their first valid delivery, daily friend-challenge state, and an immutable ledger of revive-ticket grants, spends and administrative reversals. The same anonymous identity is shared between iOS and Android.

An invitation link contains an invite code and a signature; it contains no device identifier, account token, nickname, exact location or contact information. A new player who accepts an invitation and completes their first valid delivery gives the inviter 3 in-game revive tickets and receives 1; this one-time reward can be earned for any number of genuine new players, subject to anti-abuse review. Daily friend challenges give up to 3 inviter tickets and 1 invitee ticket per server day, and the same pair of friends can earn at most once per day. Revive tickets are not money, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged, transferred, traded or sold; they are used only to resume a run. Suspected abuse is retained as a risk flag for manual review; we do not apply automatic punishment based only on shared IP addresses, invitation count or speed.

There is no in-app deletion action. To request deletion of the server-side player data described above, email chinagdh@163.com with your invite code. We delete your growth data, friendships, challenge records, delivery events and ledger, remove your leaderboard account and revoke its server-issued token, delete the optional public supporter-board row and its verified tip records, and remove the associated server data. A one-way device hash and administrative audit records may be retained for 24 months for fraud prevention and are then purged; no nickname, invite code, friendship, reward history, supporter row or score remains. The Remove Ads entitlement is not revoked. You can clear local app data by deleting the app.

Endless leaderboard

The endless leaderboard is optional and does not require an account or social login. On first use, the game creates an anonymous leaderboard account from a SHA-256 hash of the device identifier (IDFV on iOS), the app identifier and the platform. The server stores the hash, a server-issued token, a server-generated anonymous display name, your best endless score (deliveries, gates and distance), your country code and the platform. The raw device identifier is not sent or stored. Custom display-name editing is disabled in version 1.0; the generated name is display-only. You can request deletion of your leaderboard score and account by emailing chinagdh@163.com. This leaderboard is separate from Game Center and does not require an Apple ID.

Game Center

On iOS, the game can show an optional Game Center leaderboard for the same endless best score. Game Center sign-in is optional and never required to play. If you sign in, Apple’s GameKit service authenticates your Game Center account and receives the score you submit. The game does not access, store or send your Game Center profile data (such as player ID, display name, email, contacts or Apple ID); it only asks GameKit to authenticate and report the best score. Apple associates the score with your Game Center account and processes it under Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage Game Center in iOS Settings. Deleting the game’s own data does not delete data held by Apple.

Hosting of this policy

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information such as IP address and user agent under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The game does not send your game data to GitHub.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no chat, free-form posts or browser. The optional leaderboard stores only an anonymous account, a short generated display name and an endless score.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can play without any purchase, Game Center sign-in or cross-platform leaderboard use.
  • You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can change the in-game language, sound and haptics at any time.
  • You can use the Privacy Options entry when Google UMP requires one.
  • You can request deletion of your server-side player data by emailing chinagdh@163.com with your invite code. This removes friendships, invitation attribution, daily challenge records, delivery events, the revive-ticket ledger, the leaderboard account and the optional supporter-board row and tip records; a one-way device hash may be retained for 24 months for fraud prevention. The Remove Ads entitlement is kept.
  • You can clear local data by deleting the app.
  • For privacy questions, contact chinagdh@163.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will change.

Apple/Google 계정 정보, 결제 정보, 영수증, 거래 ID, 연락처, 채팅 내용을 Firebase로 전송하지 않습니다. Firebase는 앱 범위 설치 식별자와 운영 진단 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. "개인정보 옵션"을 변경하면 수집 상태가 즉시 다시 판단됩니다.

친구, 초대 및 부활권

"친구와 보상"은 선택 기능이며 소셜 로그인이 아닙니다. 전화번호, 이메일, 연락처, 소셜 계정을 요구하지 않습니다. 처음 사용할 때 서버에 익명 플레이어 ID를 하나 만들고 초대 코드, 친구 관계, 누가 누구를 초대했는지, 신규 플레이어가 첫 유효 배달을 완료했는지, 일일 친구 도전 상태, 부활권 지급·사용·관리자 취소의 불변 원장을 저장합니다. 같은 익명 ID는 iOS와 Android에서 공유됩니다.

초대 링크에는 초대 코드와 서명만 포함되며 기기 ID, 계정 토큰, 닉네임, 정확한 위치, 연락처는 포함되지 않습니다. 신규 플레이어가 초대를 수락하고 첫 유효 배달을 완료하면 초대한 사람에게 게임 내 부활권 3장, 신규 플레이어에게 1장이 지급됩니다. 이 일회성 보상은 부정 사용 검토를 조건으로 실제 신규 플레이어 수에 제한 없이 적용됩니다. 일일 친구 도전은 서버 날짜 기준 초대한 사람 최대 3장, 초대받은 사람 1장이며 같은 친구 쌍은 하루에 한 번만 받을 수 있습니다. 부활권은 화폐가 아니며 현금 가치가 없고 교환·양도·거래·판매할 수 없습니다. 플레이를 이어가는 데만 사용됩니다. 부정 사용이 의심되면 수동 검토용 위험 플래그로 보관하며, 공유 IP, 초대 수, 속도만으로 자동 처벌하지 않습니다.

앱 내 삭제 기능은 없습니다. 위 서버 측 플레이어 데이터를 삭제하려면 초대 코드를 포함하여 chinagdh@163.com으로 이메일을 보내 주세요. 성장 데이터, 친구 관계, 도전 기록, 배달 이벤트, 원장을 삭제하고 순위표 계정을 제거하며 서버 발급 토큰을 취소하고, 선택형 공개 후원자 보드 행과 검증된 팁 기록을 삭제하며 관련 서버 데이터를 제거합니다. 부정 방지를 위해 단방향 기기 해시와 관리 감사 기록을 24개월 보관한 뒤 삭제할 수 있습니다. 닉네임, 초대 코드, 친구 관계, 보상 내역, 후원자 행, 점수는 남지 않습니다. "광고 제거" 권한은 취소되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 로컬 데이터가 지워집니다.

무한 순위표

무한 순위표는 선택 기능이며 계정이나 소셜 로그인이 필요하지 않습니다. 처음 사용할 때 기기 식별자(iOS는 IDFV), 앱 식별자, 플랫폼의 SHA-256 해시로 익명 순위표 계정을 만듭니다. 서버는 해시, 서버 발급 토큰, 서버 생성 익명 표시 이름, 최고 무한 점수(배달 수, 게이트 수, 거리), 국가 코드, 플랫폼을 저장합니다. 원본 기기 식별자는 전송되거나 저장되지 않습니다. 버전 1.0에서는 표시 이름 편집이 비활성화되어 있으며 생성된 이름은 표시용입니다. 순위표 점수와 계정 삭제는 chinagdh@163.com으로 이메일을 보내 요청할 수 있습니다. 이 순위표는 Game Center와 별개이며 Apple ID가 필요하지 않습니다.

Game Center

iOS에서는 같은 무한 최고 점수를 위한 Game Center 순위표를 선택적으로 표시할 수 있습니다. Game Center 로그인은 선택 사항이며 필수가 아닙니다. 로그인하면 Apple의 GameKit 서비스가 Game Center 계정을 인증하고 제출한 점수를 받습니다. 게임은 Game Center 프로필 정보(플레이어 ID, 표시 이름, 이메일, 연락처, Apple ID 등)를 접근·저장·전송하지 않으며, GameKit에 인증과 최고 점수 보고만 요청합니다. Apple은 점수를 Game Center 계정과 연결하고 Apple의 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 처리합니다. Game Center는 iOS 설정에서 관리할 수 있습니다. 게임 자체 데이터를 삭제해도 Apple이 보유한 데이터는 삭제되지 않습니다.
