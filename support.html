Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened. Do not send Apple ID, password, payment card details or an App Store receipt to a public issue.

The rider will not move

He moves automatically. Press and hold on the side of the rider opposite the gap, then release to charge the side-bounce.

I keep missing the gap

The side you press decides the direction; hold time decides the distance. A new press can take over an unfinished hop. Pressing the rider's left sends him right; pressing his right sends him left.

No music or voices

Check the device mute switch and media volume. Music starts on the title screen and crossfades between districts. The game uses recorded/original voices, not system text-to-speech.

Purchases

Developer tips are optional and give no gameplay advantage, no unlocks and no extra lives. If a StoreKit purchase is pending or fails, retry after the App Store connection returns. The local/WebGL preview never charges; the App Store build uses Apple In-App Purchase. Do not send receipts to a public issue.

Save data

The eight-hour clock, language, best run and ending/achievement state are local to the device. Deleting the app removes them. The current build does not operate a global supporter board.
